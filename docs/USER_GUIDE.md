# User Guide

**Kuventory v2.0.0**

Welcome to Kuventory, the dedicated inventory management system for Kape Uno Bistro. This guide will walk you through the primary daily operations.

## Navigation Basics
- **Sidebar**: The main navigation menu on the left (or accessible via the hamburger menu on mobile). Use this to navigate between modules.
- **Search (⌘K)**: Press Command+K (or Ctrl+K) to open the global search modal to quickly jump between pages.
- **Theme Toggle**: Located in the top right, use the Sun/Moon icon to toggle between Light and Dark mode.

## 1. Dashboard
The Dashboard is your control center. It displays a real-time summary of today's sales, expenses, and transaction counts. The chart visually compares recent sales against expenses to give a quick overview of financial health.

## 2. Managing Inventory
The Inventory module is the core of Kuventory, structured around your daily shifts.
- **Beginning Stock**: The stock level at the start of the day.
- **Added Stock**: Any new deliveries received during the day.
- **Total Stock**: Automatically calculated by adding `Beginning` and `Added`. You cannot edit this manually.
- **AM Sales & PM Sales**: Enter the number of units consumed or sold during the morning and afternoon shifts.
- **Ending Stock**: Automatically calculated based on total sales deducted from the Total Stock.

## 3. Recording Sales
To record a customer transaction:
1. Navigate to **Sales**.
2. Click **Record Sale**.
3. Enter the customer details and select the inventory items sold.
4. If applicable, apply a discount (e.g., Senior Citizen, Employee).
5. The system automatically calculates the Net Amount. Save the record.

## 4. Recording Expenses
Operational purchases (like emergency supplies, cleaning materials, etc.) should be logged here.
1. Navigate to **Expenses**.
2. Click **Record Expense**.
3. Select the expense category and enter the amount.
4. (Optional) Provide a reference or receipt number for auditing.

## 5. Reports & Exports
At the end of the day or month, you can export your data for accounting purposes.
1. Navigate to **Reports**.
2. Select the report type (Inventory, Sales, Expenses).
3. Click the **Export** dropdown button.
4. Choose **Export as PDF** for a printable document or **Export as Excel** for spreadsheet analysis.
